# Cleopatra October — Physical Therapy Equipment Guide

A static educational website prototype for the Physical Therapy Department.

## Research-informed content
The device pages were rewritten using current/recent clinical guidance, systematic reviews, consensus statements, NHS information, and manufacturer/manual sources where available.

Important examples:
- Lumbar traction: APTA/JOSPT 2021 low-back-pain guideline; traction is not presented as a universal treatment.
- CPM: recent systematic reviews/meta-analyses; CPM is not presented as routinely superior to standard/active rehabilitation.
- TECAR: systematic review/meta-analysis and evidence review; claims are intentionally cautious because evidence is heterogeneous/limited.
- Laser and electrical stimulation: evidence-based safety review plus 2024 SAFE PAMP consensus.
- Shockwave: ISMST consensus plus 2024 international Delphi recommendations and 2025 APTA advisory.
- Isokinetic: recent systematic reviews emphasizing protocol consistency and measurement limitations.
- Balance: manufacturer/manual sources are used only for device workflow concepts; exact model-specific normative values are not invented.

## Device-model limitations
Two entries require confirmation before device-specific operating instructions are published:
1. "Traction ISIS" — the exact manufacturer/model has not been verified.
2. "TCAR" — this prototype treats it as TECAR because that is the most plausible physiotherapy meaning, but the exact device/model must be confirmed.

Until the exact model is confirmed, the site deliberately avoids fixed force, energy, frequency, dose, ROM, or timing recipes.

## Clinical governance
This is an educational interface, not a treatment prescription. Before internal/public release, have the Physical Therapy Department/HOD and relevant clinical governance team review:
- contraindications and precautions
- device-specific parameters
- postoperative restrictions
- emergency procedures
- infection-control/cleaning instructions
- manufacturer manuals
- local hospital protocols
